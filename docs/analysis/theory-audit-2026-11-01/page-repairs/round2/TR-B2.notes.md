# TR-B2 round-2 claim verdicts

## tr-b2-compound-past-hikaye
- #1 **overstated** (validator partly wrong). G&K §8.4(iii): when a form has a position-3 suffix and a copula, the 3pl -lAr's "preferred position is between these two, but it may also come at the end" (kat-sa-lar-dı / kat-sa-ydı-lar). G&K §8.3 also cites anlattırmalıydılar. gitmiştiler is therefore grammatical, not non-standard. The page already ranks gitmişlerdi first ("more common in writing"). No change. (See the TR-B1 notes for the conflicting `*geliyordular` on tr-b1-past-continuous-iyordu.)

## tr-b2-converb-until
- #1 **real**. *Buraya … kalmak* uses the wrong case; kal- takes the locative. Target is now "==Ölene kadar== burada kalmak istiyordu." The gloss and note are unchanged and still match.
- #2 **real**. "uyuyunca-ya" → "uyuyuncaya".
- #3 **real** (self-contradiction). "indivisible" / "unanalysable" are softened to "best learned as a single fixed suffix (historically -mAk + -sIz + -In)" and "Learn the suffix as a fixed unit". The formation line now says "Its -mAk- is the infinitive suffix" (it was "-mA- comes from the infinitive -mAk"). The ✗ *yapmasızın stays.

## tr-b2-reported-questions
- #1 **real**. The note gave the accusative twice as "-nu", contracted to -nu. Rewritten to fit the page's table segmentation (-in-i) and G&K §8.1 (3sg possessive -(s)I(n), with n before a case suffix): ol-duğ-un-u = -DIK + possessive -un + accusative -u.
- #2 **real**. "Same rule applies to the affirmative stem in the -Ip frame" contradicted the formation and pitfalls. Now: the choice applies to the negative branch (gelip gelmediğini vs. gelip gelmeyeceğini), and the -Ip branch carries no tense.
- #3 **overstated**. "Possessor of the action" is a common learner gloss for the genitive subject of a nominalised clause. G&K treats genitive subject + possessive agreement as a possessive construction (§24). No change.
- #4 **real** (minor). The accusative bullet now says -I after the possessive, with -n- between after the 3rd-person possessive (gel-diğ-im-i vs gel-diğ-in-i). The formation lines keep "-(y)I" as the citation name.
