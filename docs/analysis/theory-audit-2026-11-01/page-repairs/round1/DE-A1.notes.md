# DE-A1 patch notes

13 pages patched; all 15 findings fixed, none skipped. Verified against Durrell where noted.

## de-a1-accusative
- "noun itself does not change" (weak masculines): **fixed**. The sentence now says the noun *usually* does not change and names the weak-masculine exception (*Ich kenne Herrn Müller*, *den Studenten*).

## de-a1-dative
- Self-contradictory *Eltern* note: **fixed**. The example note now says *Eltern* already ends in -n, so the dative plural adds nothing. The *meinen* part is unchanged.

## de-a1-imperative
- "Optional -e" for all regular verbs: **fixed** (Durrell §10.2.1 f(i), g(iii) confirm). The callout item now says the -e is required after -t/-d, -ig and consonant + m/n (*Arbeite! Warte! Entschuldige! Öffne!*, not *\*Arbeit!*). It keeps the optional case and the "no -e after e → i(e)" rule. The du-row of the table now reads "stem (drop -en; + -e after -t/-d)".

## de-a1-modal-verbs-present
- *\*Ich kann das Meer sehen* starred: **fixed**. The perception-verb pitfall is replaced with a real error: conjugating the final infinitive (*\*Er muss heute arbeitet*). This matches the curriculum, which removed the same commonError.

## de-a1-noun-gender
- "-ung … no exceptions": **fixed** (Durrell §1.1.2a, *der Sprung*). The pitfall now covers nouns *formed with* the suffixes -ung/-heit/-keit and warns about suffixless *der Sprung*. The *Wohnung* example note now says "(as in wohnen → Wohnung)" to tie the rule to the suffix.

## de-a1-numbers-ordinals
- Years only in hundreds: **fixed** (Durrell §8, "years since the millennium are given with zweitausend … 2021 is zweitausendeinundzwanzig"). Three places changed: the intro, the "Years" list item and the tausend pitfall. They now limit the hundreds reading to 1100–1999 and add *zweitausendeinundzwanzig*. The pitfall's wrong form is now *\*eintausendneunhundertachtundneunzig*.
- "do not add a space before the month name": **fixed**. The text now says a space still follows the dot (Durrell: *am 5. Juni*).
- *\*der siebente* starred: **fixed** (Durrell §8: "das siebente is less frequent, but often used in eastern Germany and Austria"). The pitfall now stars *\*der dreite* and *\*der einte* and notes that *der siebente* is a less common but correct form.

## de-a1-personal-pronouns
- "ihn is the only 3sg form that differs acc/dat": **fixed**. The callout now says *ihn* is the only 3sg accusative that differs from its nominative, and that all three dative forms change (ihm, ihm, ihr).

## de-a1-plural-formation
- "always plural after a number": **fixed** (Durrell ch. 1 measure nouns). The "After a number word" item and the pitfall now both name the exception: masculine/neuter units of money and measure stay singular (*zwei Euro, drei Kilo, zwei Glas Bier*). The page now agrees with the numbers page (*einundzwanzig Euro*).

## de-a1-possessive-articles
- "use where the noun is not yet known to the listener": **fixed**. The item is renamed "Taking the place of the article". It says possessives fill the article slot and take *ein*-endings but are specific in meaning, so they work for known referents (*Wo ist mein Schlüssel?*).

## de-a1-praeteritum-sein-haben
- "Perfekt sounds unnatural / clumsy; es gab the only natural option": **fixed** (Durrell §12.2.2a(i): *war* and *bin gewesen* are "equally frequent" in North German speech, and the past tense is rare in the south). Three places changed:
  - Intro: *war/hatte* are much more common than other verbs' Präteritum and safe everywhere, and the Perfekt is also correct and common, especially in the south, Austria and Switzerland.
  - *es gab* note: "the usual choice", and *hat es gegeben* is also heard.
  - Pitfall: reframed as "Treating the Perfekt as wrong — or forming it wrongly", with a real error (*\*ich habe krank gewesen*).

## de-a1-present-irregular
- Garbled *werden* description: **fixed**. The formation line now says du and er both change e → i, du also loses the d (*wirst*), and er takes no -t (*wird*). The example note now says the vowel changes and there is no -t ending (not *\*wirdt*).

## de-a1-temporal-prepositions
- "von … bis | dative": **fixed** (Durrell ch. 18 *bis*). The table case cell now reads "von + dative; bis + accusative, or + a second preposition (bis zum Abend)".
- *\*für den ganzen Tag bleiben* starred: **fixed**. The pitfall now covers adding a preposition to bare accusative phrases, with *\*in nächste Woche* as the real error. It states that *den ganzen Tag bleiben* is the most common choice and that *für den ganzen Tag bleiben* is also correct for a planned duration. This matches the curriculum, which removed the für commonError.

## de-a1-zero-article
- *\*Wir kaufen einige Äpfel* starred: **fixed**. The starred form is now *\*Wir kaufen eine Äpfel*, with a note that there is no plural *ein* and that *einige Äpfel* is also correct but means "a few".
