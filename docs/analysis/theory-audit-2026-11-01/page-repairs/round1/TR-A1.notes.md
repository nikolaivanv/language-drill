# TR-A1 theory patch notes

All 23 findings across 16 pages verified (G&K where available) and fixed; none skipped.

## tr-a1-beri-dir
- saattan → saatten (error): fixed. Pitfall now flags *iki saattan beri as the wrong form, explains saat takes front suffixes (cf. saattir, G&K §3.4(iv)) → saatten / iki saatten beri; the için pitfall's alternative now reads iki saatten beri bekliyorum.
- "predicate is always ongoing" (misleading): fixed. "always" → "normally", and added a negative -DI example (İki yıldır onu görmedim); intro sentence softened to "normally pair" (G&K §17.2.3(ii)).
- sabahtan beri gloss: fixed. Changed to "since (this) morning".

## tr-a1-accusative-definite-object
- "two-way" harmony: fixed → "four-way" (matches the table).
- "and other pronouns": fixed → "personal pronouns (and demonstrative pronouns like bu, şu)", plus "Indefinite pronouns stay bare: Bir şey aldım."

## tr-a1-ablative-dative
- "last consonant" callout: fixed → "if the stem ends in a voiceless consonant … if it ends in a vowel or a voiced consonant (masa → masadan), it starts with d".

## tr-a1-dan-a-kadar
- "Both halves required" / "Dropping kadar" pitfall: fixed. Intro sentence now says -A kadar can stand alone (cumaya kadar) and the pair works without kadar (sabahtan akşama). Pitfall reframed as "Thinking kadar is obligatory", with the G&K §17.2.2 ex. (30) "Cumaya kadar biter" for when kadar is needed.
- Day-name apostrophe: fixed. Example target → Pazartesiden cumaya kadar; note now says day names take no apostrophe.

## tr-a1-clock-time-dates
- "Days never take a case suffix": fixed in all three places (intro, when-to-use, pitfall), now limited to "on <day>". The pitfall notes pazartesiden beri / cumaya kadar are fine.

## tr-a1-genitive-possessive
- "Neither suffix can be dropped" + starred *öğretmen kitabı: fixed. The intro now says only the possessive is obligatory: dropping the genitive gives an indefinite compound, and a pronoun possessor is often dropped (kitabı). The pitfall is reframed as "Omitting the genitive when you mean a specific possessor", with the star removed and the compound called valid but different in meaning.

## tr-a1-locative

## tr-a1-postpositions-once-sonra

## tr-a1-vowel-harmony
- Reversed loanword pitfall (oteller/lokallar): fixed. Rewritten per G&K §3.4(iv): back-vowel loanwords taking front suffixes (saatler not *saatlar, halim not *halım, golü), dictionaries mark them (saat (-ti)).

## tr-a1-instrumental-ile
- onlarla + colloquial benle/onla: fixed. Formation now says onlar takes -lA directly (onlarla) and benle/senle/onla are casual. Added an onlar | — | onlarla | with them row to the pronoun table. The example note "not *onla" → "casual speech also has onla". Pitfall reframed as "Mis-forming pronoun + -(y)lA": genitive for ben/sen/o/biz/siz, onlarla not *onlarınla (a genuinely wrong form), and bare benle/senle/onla marked colloquial, not starred (G&K §8.1.4, §6).

## tr-a1-gore-bence

## tr-a1-future
