# DE-B2 theory patch notes

All 29 findings in 16 pages were re-verified, most against Durrell. All 29 are fixed and none were skipped. The patch applies cleanly (`ok` on every page, with no mixed-script warnings).

## de-b2-dass-equivalents
- Obligatory correlate / false ✗ on *Wir freuen uns, dass …* (§16.5.14: "never incorrect to include it"; sich freuen auf/über, hoffen auf and aufpassen auf are in the "often left out" list) → **fixed**. The pitfall now targets *sich verlassen auf* (✗ *Ich verlasse mich, dass …*). The correlates bullet, the table note and the example note now say the correlate is optional with sich freuen and adds emphasis.
- "Modal verbs do not take zu" → **fixed**. The sentence now reads: zu goes before the modal infinitive (*Ich hoffe, dich bald wiedersehen zu können*).
- "zu-infinitive — identical subjects only" → **fixed**. The understood subject can be the main-clause subject or object (*Ich bitte dich, früher zu kommen*). This is updated in what-is-it, both when-to-use bullets and the pitfall, which is retitled "for an unrelated subject". Its ✗ example stays because it is genuinely wrong. Matches the curriculum fix (§11.2.3a).

## de-b2-fixed-prepositions
- Correlate "required, cannot be omitted"; ✗ *Er ist stolz, dass …* → **fixed** (§6.4.1c "equally acceptable and grammatical"). The callout now says the correlate is very common, never wrong, and often optional. In the intro it now "usually" stands. The example note says it is optional with stolz. The pitfall is reframed to a genuine error, a bare preposition before dass (✗ *Er ist stolz auf, dass …*).

## de-b2-indefinite-pronouns
- irgendein determiner vs pronoun endings → **fixed** (§5.5.11d). The formation text now says that as a determiner it declines exactly like ein (*irgendein Mann/Buch*) and that the table shows the pronoun *irgendeiner*. The callout now says *irgendein* is the only correct determiner form (✗ *irgendeines Buch*).
- welche for all mass nouns → **fixed** (§5.5.26). Gender agreement (Käse → welchen, Milch → welche, Brot → welches) is added in when-to-use, formation, the example note and the pitfall. The table column "pl. / mass noun" is now "pl.".
- "einige always plural" → **fixed** (§5.5.7a). Only mehrere is always plural; einig- has singular uses (*vor einiger Zeit, mit einigem Glück*). The formation sentence is softened to match.

## de-b2-konjunktiv-i
- "3sg always ends in -e" → **fixed**. It now ends in -e, "the one exception is sei".

## de-b2-konjunktiv-ii
- Präteritum stems konn-/durf-/soll-/woll- → **fixed**. They are now konnt-/durft-/sollt-/wollt-.
- "umlaut added where possible" → **fixed** (§10.5.2a). Umlaut goes on strong verbs, haben/werden/wissen and können/müssen/dürfen/mögen. Weak verbs and sollen/wollen keep the Präteritum form.

## de-b2-mittelfeld-word-order
- Clausal nicht "after all adverbials and objects" → **fixed** (§19.6a). nicht now comes after noun objects and most adverbials, but before manner adverbials and verb complements (*nicht nach Berlin, nicht auf ihn, nicht schnell*). This is updated in what-is-it, the when-to-use bullet and the slot-10 table row.
- Pitfall "at least one object is a full NP" → **fixed**. It now reads "both objects are full noun phrases".
- Table presented the accusative NP before the adverbials as canonical → **fixed** (§19.5.1). The intro now says "typical … strong tendency, not a rigid rule". Slot 5 notes that by default a noun accusative object follows most adverbials (*gestern im Büro den Bericht gelesen*). The table structure is unchanged.

## de-b2-modal-particles-advanced
- "One particle per clause" callout → **fixed** (§9.1.6, §9.1.22). The callout is rewritten: combinations are frequent (*doch mal, ja schon, denn eigentlich*), follow a fairly fixed order, and should be learned as chunks.
- nur = "mild caution" vs bloß → **fixed** (§9.1.5, §9.1.25). Warning nur ≈ bloß, with bloß slightly more emphatic and colloquial. This is updated in the bloß bullet and the example note. The pitfall is reframed to the genuine contrast: bloß cannot carry the reassuring nur (*Kommen Sie nur herein!*). Matches the curriculum fix.

## de-b2-modal-perfect-word-order
- "hätte (or wäre … rare with modals)" → **fixed**. The auxiliary is always hätte (*Er hätte kommen sollen*, not *wäre kommen sollen*).

## de-b2-nominalization
- -heit/-keit row sourced from "verb" → **fixed**. It is now "adjective". The intro now says "many verbs and adjectives" get suffix nouns.
- "strong adjective endings after an article" → **fixed**. The intro now says weak after der/das and strong after ein or with no article. The formation sentence adds *ein Kranker, etwas Gutes*.
- "always singular, no plural" → **fixed** (§11.4). The text now says they are normally singular, with a few lexicalized plurals (*die Schreiben*, *die Essen*).

## de-b2-noun-verb-collocations
- *sich Mühe geben* / *Bescheid geben* "no single-verb equivalent" → **fixed**. The set-phrase examples are now *eine Rolle spielen, in Frage kommen*, and the bullet notes *sich Mühe geben ≈ sich bemühen*, *Bescheid geben ≈ benachrichtigen*.

## de-b2-passive-alternatives
- sein + zu possibility "occasional" → **fixed** (§13.4.5a). Obligation and possibility are now both main readings (*Er ist nicht zu erreichen, kaum zu glauben*). The pitfall is retitled "as one meaning only" and covers both directions. Matches the curriculum fix.

## de-b2-relatives-advanced
- ✗ *Wer zu spät kommt, der muss …* → **fixed** (§5.4.5a). With matching cases the resumptive is now described as optional and emphatic (*Wer A sagt, der muss auch B sagen*), in when-to-use, formation and the example note. The pitfall is replaced with a genuine error, a resumptive in the wrong case (✗ *Wer das glaubt, der kann ich nicht helfen* → *dem*).
- wo(r)- with "indefinite/abstract noun" antecedents → **fixed** (§5.4.4b). wo(r)- is now for clauses and was-type words (alles, etwas, nichts, das, superlatives). With a noun antecedent the page says to use preposition + der/die/das (*das Problem, über das wir sprachen*). The intro is updated to match.

## de-b2-subjective-modals
- *Er will alles wissen* (volitional reading) → **fixed**. The present-reference example is now *Er will den Täter nicht kennen*, where the claim reading is the natural one.

## de-b2-text-reference-words
- *der solche Fehler* "rare" → **fixed** (§5.1.6). The row is now *ein solcher Fehler* (such a mistake). The prose says solch- takes adjective endings after ein/kein/quantifiers (*jeder solche Gedanke, alle solchen Fehler*) and strong endings with no article, and that *der solche Fehler* is wrong.
- Sentence anaphor "has no inflection" → **fixed**. It becomes *dem* with dative verbs (*Dem stimme ich zu*, not *Das stimme ich zu*) and a da(r)-compound after prepositions. Updated in formation and when-to-use.

## de-b2-verb-prefixes
- Literal/figurative rule for variable prefixes → **fixed** (§20.7.6). Separable now means the prefix keeps its adverbial sense (um- = turning over or changing). Inseparable now means a lexicalized meaning, often figurative or, for um-, encircling. The stress callout and both umfahren example notes now say both readings are literal. The participle heuristic in the pitfall drops "= literal/figurative".

## de-b2-word-formation
- Invented *freundig* / *kinderfreudig* → **fixed**. Replaced with the real pair *geistig* (mental) vs *geistlich* (spiritual, religious). The täglich/-tägig pair is kept.
- -keit only after -lich/-ig → **fixed** (§20.2.1). -keit also goes after -bar/-sam and most -el/-er adjectives (*Dankbarkeit, Einsamkeit*), and -igkeit after -haft/-los (*Arbeitslosigkeit*). The pitfall adds "not wholly regular". The table base-type cells are updated to match.
