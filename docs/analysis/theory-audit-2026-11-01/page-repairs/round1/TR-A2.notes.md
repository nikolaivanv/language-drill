## tr-a2-correlative-conjunctions
- ne…ne must have a positive verb: **fixed**. G&K §20.4 (ex. 39/40) confirms both predicates are possible. "When to use it" now says either is correct and the two differ only in stress. ## tr-a2-aorist
- "*Her sabah kahve içiyorum implies right now": **fixed**. G&K §21.3.2 confirms -Iyor is also habitual. The pitfall is rewritten to "Using -(I)yor for general truths": *içiyorum* is correct as a current habit, while *Kaplumbağa yavaş yürüyor* means "is walking (now)", so a general statement takes *yürür*. This matches the curriculum commonError. The example note for *Her sabah… içerim* now adds that *içiyorum* is also correct.
