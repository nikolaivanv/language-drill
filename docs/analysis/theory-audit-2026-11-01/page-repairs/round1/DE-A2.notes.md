# DE-A2 patch notes

All 20 patched pages apply `ok` with no mixed-script warnings. Book = Durrell (Hammer).

## de-a2-adjective-declension-indefinite
- ein-word "has an ending" self-checks (error) → fixed. Rewrote the what-is-it sentence, the rule-of-thumb callout and the warn self-check: an ein-word with an added ending → -en, but -e after *eine* (fem. nom./acc.). A bare ein-word (ein, kein, mein, unser) → strong -er/-es. Added the note that the -er of unser/euer is part of the word (*unser alter Tisch*, *unser kleines Kind*). The table intro now mentions the -e after eine as well (§6.1.3, Table 6.5).
- -el/-er stem -e- (misleading) → fixed. The rule now covers -el adjectives always and -er adjectives only after au/eu (teuer, sauer), and adds that other -er adjectives usually keep the -e- (*ein sauberes Zimmer*). This matches the curriculum fix (§6.1.5).

## de-a2-akkusativ-prepositions
- bis + article (misleading) → fixed. The bis note now says bis is never followed by an article, stands alone before names and time phrases, and otherwise needs a second preposition (*bis zum Bahnhof*, not *\*bis den Bahnhof*). The der→den pitfall now notes bis as the exception (§18.1.1, checked).

## de-a2-comparison
- -est rule vs groß (error) → fixed. The rule now says "usually", names groß as irregular (am größten) and adds that longer words with an unstressed last syllable take -sten (am spannendsten). Checked against §6.5.1(b),(d).

## de-a2-dass-clauses
- welches test (misleading) → fixed. It is now the dieses/welches test: dieses for the article or demonstrative, welches for the relative, and dass when neither fits.

## de-a2-dativ-prepositions
- "accusative plural" for den (error) → fixed. It now reads "den is the masculine accusative form, wrong after mit".
- uncontracted model sentences (misleading) → fixed. The models now read *Er arbeitet beim Bäcker* (gloss: "at the baker's") and *Ich gehe zum Arzt*.

## de-a2-demonstratives-welch
- im selben labelled informal (misleading) → fixed in all three places (when-to-use, formation, pitfalls). It is now called normal or fully standard, and *am selben Tag* is added. §5.1.5 cites *Sie wohnt im selben Haus*.

## de-a2-destination-prepositions
- "requires the accusative" incl. zu (error) → fixed. The what-is-it and formation paragraphs now say zu always takes the dative (zum = zu + dem, zur = zu + der), while in/an/auf take the accusative for direction. The "two contractions are obligatory" claim, which did not match the 4-row table, now reads "the normal forms".
- "nach and auf do not contract" (error) → fixed. Only nach is said not to contract; auf contracts with das: *aufs Land* (§4.1.1(c), table lists aufs).
- Extra consistency edit (not an audit finding): removed "der Iran" from the "countries that always use an article" pitfall. The corrected curriculum (de-b1-articles-use) makes the article with Iran optional. *in den Iran* elsewhere on the page is still correct.

## de-a2-indefinite-pronouns-basic
- alle/alles "do not change form" (misleading) → fixed. The page now says etwas and nichts never change, while alles and alle decline, with the common datives *mit allem* and *mit allen*.

## de-a2-nicht-sondern
- quick test contradicts page (misleading) → fixed. The test is now "does the second part replace the negated element ('not X, but Y instead')? → sondern; contrast with the whole clause, with or without a negation → aber". This matches the page's own warn callout.

## de-a2-past-participle-formation
- inseparable prefixes "carry the stress" (error) → fixed. The page now says they are unstressed and the stress falls on the stem (be-SU-chen), so, as with -ieren verbs, there is no ge- (§10.2.1).
- "-et after consonant cluster" (error) → fixed. The table row now reads "stem ends in -t/-d, or in -m/-n after a consonant other than l/r (öffnen → geöffnet)" (§10.2.1(a)(iii)).

## de-a2-perfekt-with-haben
- *das Film* (error) → fixed to *Habt ihr den Film gesehen?*

## de-a2-perfekt-with-sein
- Wohin/Woher test (misleading) → fixed. The test now applies when "the subject itself moves". A sentence was added saying that verbs which move an object take haben (*Wohin hast du das Buch gelegt?*). This agrees with the page's existing haben-with-object note.

## de-a2-praeteritum-modals
- mögen g → ch exception (error) → fixed in both the what-is-it and formation prose, now consistent with the table (mochte, not *\*mogte*).
- mochte as a synonym of wollte (error) → fixed. The page now says "use wollte (it is also the past of möchte)". Added that mochte means "liked" (*Ich mochte den Film*), not "wanted to".

## de-a2-quantifiers-other
- capital letter in etwas/nichts anderes (error) → fixed. It now reads "stays lowercase" (§21.2).

## de-a2-reflexive-verbs
- mich/dich called the "unique" forms (error) → fixed. The callout now says sich (3rd person and Sie) is the only special reflexive form, and mich/dich/uns/euch are the ordinary personal pronouns.
- sich treffen as obligatorily reflexive (error) → fixed. It is replaced by *sich erholen* (to recover / relax). The reciprocal *Wir treffen uns* example stays.
- "directly after the finite verb" (misleading) → fixed. The formation text now says "after the finite verb … if a pronoun subject follows the verb, the reflexive comes after it". The inversion paragraph now says the reflexive follows a pronoun subject but usually precedes a noun subject, and adds *Heute freut sich mein Bruder*. This matches the curriculum fix (§19.4.1d).

## de-a2-relative-clauses-nom-acc
- "differs from the article in masc. acc." (error) → fixed. The page now says the forms are identical to the definite article in every form; as with the article, only the masculine changes between the two cases (der/den).

## de-a2-seit-present
- negation alone triggers the Perfekt (misleading) → fixed. In both what-is-it and when-to-use, the Perfekt is now tied to an event that has not happened (*Ich habe ihn seit Jahren nicht gesehen*), and a continuing negated state stays present (*Ich rauche seit zwei Jahren nicht mehr*). The list-item title changed to "Events that have not happened" (§12.1.2(b), curriculum fix).

## de-a2-weil-deshalb
- weil fixes the order to result first (misleading) → fixed. The when-to-use item, the table cell ("after clause 1 (or before it)") and the "Direction matters" callout now say a weil-clause usually follows but may come first (*Weil ich krank bin, bleibe ich zu Hause*). Only denn always follows.

## de-a2-wissen-kennen
- "wissen cannot take a noun phrase" (error) → fixed everywhere it appeared: what-is-it, when-to-use, the formation sentence-structure line, the pitfall (retitled "wissen for people, places and things", now framed as information vs acquaintance, with *Ich weiß die Antwort* / *Weißt du seine Adresse?* as correct) and the "reliable rule" warn callout. *\*Ich weiß den Film* stays as the genuine error. This matches the curriculum fix.
- Broken quote markup in what-is-it → fixed. It now reads `"to know": wissen …`.

## de-a2-wenn-als
- Broken callout markup → fixed. It now reads `If you can only say "at the time when" (one single occasion in the past), use als.`
