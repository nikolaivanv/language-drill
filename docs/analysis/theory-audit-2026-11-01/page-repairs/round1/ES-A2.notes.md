# ES-A2 theory patch notes

All 16 findings across 14 pages are fixed. None were skipped. Every page applies `ok`, with no mixed-script warnings.

## es-a2-por-para
- Self-contradictory contraction callout: **fixed**. The callout now says neither por nor para contracts with el (por el, para el). Only a and de contract (al, del).

## es-a2-periphrases-obligation-aspect
- hay que "invariable regardless of tense": **fixed** (B&B §25.4.2; matches the curriculum variant). The callout now says hay que does not change for person but does change for tense (había que, hubo que, habrá que). The when-to-use line "Hay never changes form" became "never changes for person, only for tense (había que, habrá que)". The formation line "only one form for all persons" was already correct and is unchanged. The `*lo hay que hacer` pitfall is kept, per the audit's own note.

## es-a2-indirect-object-pronouns-se
- se le ≠ dative of possession: **fixed** (B&B §9.3.4, §30.4.1; matches the curriculum fix).
  - What-is-it: the dative of possession is now attributed to le/les («le robaron la cartera»). The page adds that the se in «se le cayó el vaso» belongs to caerse and is not le → se.
  - When-to-use: the bullet now teaches le/les + definite article («le robaron la cartera»). «se le cayó…» is presented as the pronominal-verb case.
  - The note on the «Se le cayó el vaso a Juan» example was reworded the same way.

## es-a2-imperfect
- ver explanation: **fixed** (B&B §16, «veía … not the expected *vía»). The callout now says ver keeps the e of its infinitive (stem ve-) before the -er endings, so the form is veía and not *vía.

## es-a2-gustar-type-verbs
- "Only two (3rd-person) forms": **fixed**. The two-form rule is now scoped to subjects that are things or activities. A sentence was added saying a person can be the subject and the verb then agrees with that person: «me gustas» (I like you), «le gusto» (he/she likes me). The pitfall "the person is always an indirect object, never the subject" refers to the experiencer, so it was left as is.

## es-a2-tonic-pronouns-prepositions
- entre/según/excepto… take yo/tú: **fixed** (B&B §13.1 and note 1; §38). Changes:
  - What-is-it now says "after most prepositions".
  - The when-to-use bullet is now "After most prepositions" and lists entre, según, excepto, salvo, menos, incluso and hasta («even») as taking yo/tú, with examples («entre tú y yo», «según tú», «excepto yo»).
  - The pitfall "applies after all prepositions" now says "most" and names the same exceptions, contrasting «entre tú y yo» with «entre ti y mí».
- Reflexive sí / consigo: **fixed** (B&B §13.3). The callout now names reflexive sí as a special tonic form and says con fuses with mí, ti and sí: consigo («lo lleva consigo»).

## es-a2-estar-gerundio
- poder → pudiendo: **fixed** (B&B §16, §24.2; matches the curriculum fix). Changes:
  - The formation heading is now "(-ir verbs, plus poder)", with a line saying poder → pudiendo.
  - Row `poder | o → u | pudiendo | being able to` added to the stem-change table.
  - The pitfall now says the only common -er verb that changes is poder (not *podiendo).
  - The warn callout now says "-ir verbs (plus poder → pudiendo)". The volver → volviendo contrast is kept.

## es-a2-imperative-affirmative
- vosotros -d drops before os: **fixed** (B&B §21.2.4). "Completely regular, with no exceptions" became: no irregular verbs, but -d drops when os is attached (sentaos, levantaos), with idos (irse) as the one exception.

## es-a2-preterito-perfecto
- Peninsular norm stated as universal: **fixed** (B&B §18.2, §18.2.8; matches the curriculum fix). Changes:
  - What-is-it now says "In Spain it is the tense of choice…".
  - The ya bullet now says that in Spain it normally pairs with the perfecto, while most of Latin America uses the preterite («ya llegó»).
  - The hoy bullet now says "in Spain".
  - The pitfall is reframed as Peninsular ("(Spain)" in the heading). It still recommends «hoy he hecho» and adds that «hoy hice» / «ya llegó» are standard and correct in most of Latin America.
- "Three … irregular participles" implies only three: **fixed**. The text now says several verbs are irregular and three are shown, and lists dicho, puesto, vuelto, abierto and roto. The table is unchanged.

## es-a2-si-present-conditional
- "si never followed by future": **fixed** (B&B §29.8.1 notes 2–3; matches the curriculum fix). The rule is now scoped to si meaning «if», with a note that «whether» si can take the future (no sé si vendrá). The ✗ example *Si vendrás mañana… is a conditional si and stays wrong.

## es-a2-todo-otro-quantifiers
- todo + bare noun: **fixed** (B&B §10.17(a): todo español, en todo caso). Changes:
  - The when-to-use bullet now limits the determiner requirement to «all» / «the whole of». It adds that singular todo + bare noun means «every / any» (todo tipo de, en todo caso).
  - The formation callout and the pitfall text are scoped the same way.
  - Dependent fix: the ✗ gloss for *todo los estudiantes said "determiner missing", which was false because los is present. It now says todo does not agree with the plural noun.

## es-a2-exclamatives-impersonals
- "Both sets are impersonal": **fixed**. The sentence now says only the hacer weather expressions are truly impersonal, while the exclamatives and estamos a … grados have ordinary subjects (este restaurante, tú, nosotros).

## es-a2-diacritic-pairs
- "Accent does not change pronunciation" contradiction: **fixed**. The sentence now reads "In speech the two words differ in stress, and the accent shows the reader which word is meant", which matches the next sentence on lexical stress.

## es-a2-direct-object-pronouns
- "una idea / una cláusula (neuter) → lo": **fixed**. The table row label is now "a whole idea or clause (neuter)", keeping «Lo sé». The noun una idea would take la.
