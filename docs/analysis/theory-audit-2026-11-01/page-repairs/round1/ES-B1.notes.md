# ES-B1 theory patch notes

All 15 findings were re-checked against Butt & Benjamin; all 15 fixed, none skipped.

## es-a2-comparatives-superlatives
- menor for physical size (error): FIXED. Callout now says to use the regular forms for physical size, that mayor = "bigger" mostly in formal writing, and that menor is not used for size (*esta caja es menor que la otra). B&B §6.8a, §6.9.
- más bueno / más malo starred (misleading): FIXED. Pitfall now says "For quality, say mejor..." and adds that más bueno/más malo are correct for character (es el más malo de todos). B&B §6.3.1 n3. Matches the curriculum fix.

## es-b1-deber-obligation-probability
- "presence/absence of de changes the meaning entirely" (misleading): FIXED. The what-is-it sentence, the warn callout and the "interchangeable" pitfall now say the same thing: deber de for obligation is non-standard; plain deber for a guess (deben ser las cinco) is widespread and RAE-accepted, but deber de is preferred. B&B §25.3.2 n1. Matches the curriculum.

## es-b1-discourse-connectors
- *aunque llueva, salimos (misleading): FIXED. The star is removed. The pitfall now explains the subjunctive = "even if" or dismissing a known fact (aunque sea tu padre…). The when-to-use bullet adds the "brush a known fact aside" use. B&B §20.10 (perdieron aunque jugaran bien), §20.4.10.

## es-b1-imperative-negative-pronouns
- accent "always with one or two pronouns" (error): FIXED in the when-to-use bullet, the formation paragraph and the pitfall. An accent is needed only when the stressed syllable ends up third-from-last or earlier: díselo/dámelas/cómpralo, but dime/dale/vete/ponlo. Now consistent with the page's own table (dime, vete).

## es-b1-impersonal-plural
- "verb agrees with its object" (error): FIXED. The callout now says the verb never agrees with its object and stays 3rd plural (me robaron la cartera, venden pan).

## es-b1-indirect-questions
- "not available with si" vs. the "si ir" example (error): FIXED. The when-to-use bullet now says the infinitive works with si too (No sé si ir). Formation table row: yes/no → "finite clause (or bare infinitive, same subject)", with example "No sé si ir." Matches the curriculum.

## es-b1-influence-verbs-infinitive
- "dative clitic" / le-les with dejar/hacer + intransitive (misleading): FIXED, lightly. As the brief says, le with a causee is widely accepted. "Dative clitic" became "object clitic" (what-is-it, pattern, subtitle, example note). The formation paragraph now says: permitir/prohibir take le/les; dejar/hacer take lo/la/los/las with an intransitive infinitive (la dejaron ir, la hice reír) and le/les when the infinitive has an object (le hice tomar un café). B&B §15.6.5 n2. Matches the curriculum desc.
- clitic "dropped" with que + subjunctive (error): FIXED. The clitic can stay or be left out: le permitieron que entrara / permitieron que entrara. B&B §20.3.8(c).
- *Mis padres no dejan salir (misleading): FIXED. The star is removed and the pitfall reframed: "Dropping the clitic when a specific person is meant". Without the clitic the permission is generic (nobody is allowed out). B&B §20.3.8 n2.

## es-b1-llevar-time-expressions
- *hace un año que trabajé / trabajaba (misleading): FIXED. Both starred forms were removed. The pitfall now explains that a preterite changes the meaning to "ago" (Hace un año que trabajé aquí = I worked here a year ago). The imperfect example was dropped rather than explained: B&B §36.3.2 n3 covers it only in negative sentences, which is out of scope here. B&B §36.4.

## es-b1-nominalizers
- "del never with nominalizer el" (misleading): FIXED. The callout keeps "*elde doesn't exist" but now says de + el always contracts, including before the nominalizer el: la del que habló ayer, not *la de el que. B&B §40.1.4 n1.

## es-b1-pluperfect
- antes de que / después de que as pluperfect triggers (misleading): FIXED. Both were removed from the trigger list and nunca antes (never before) was added. The bullet also notes that antes de que always takes the subjunctive (antes de que llegara). B&B §20.4.7 n3.

## es-b1-present-subjunctive
- "o→u in all forms" and -ir e→ie with no nosotros change (error): FIXED. The paragraph is rewritten: -ar/-er e→ie, o→ue change in all forms except nosotros/vosotros; -ir stem-changers also change in nosotros/vosotros (e→i: sintamos, pidamos; o→u: durmamos). A dormir (o→ue/u) column was added to the stem-change table. B&B §16.6.

## es-b1-reported-speech
- commands always que + present subjunctive (error): FIXED in the what-is-it paragraph, the when-to-use bullet (Dice que te sientes / Dijo que te sentaras), the formation table (split into present vs. past reporting verb rows: dice que vengas / dijo que vinieras), the formation structure line, the infinitive pitfall (dijo que hiciéramos…) and the example note ("Even with" → "With a present-tense reporting verb"). B&B §20.3.2.

## es-b1-ser-estar-uses
- "atmospheric conditions" with impersonal ser (misleading): FIXED. Now reads "time of day and day/night" in what-is-it and the bullet heading. The bullet adds that weather takes hacer/estar (hace frío, está nublado).
