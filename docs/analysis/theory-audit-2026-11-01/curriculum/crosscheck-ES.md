## Curriculum-only errors

High-confidence errors in the prompt-injected fields of the affected points that the audit did not flag:

1. **es-b1-deber-obligation-probability, L4706 (commonError):** "Using the plain present of deber for advice instead of the softened conditional deberías ('*debes hacer ejercicio' as advice instead of 'deberías hacer ejercicio')." *Debes hacer ejercicio* is fully grammatical. It is a stronger, more direct recommendation (B&B ch. 25 treats present *deber* as plain 'must/ought'). The asterisk teaches the generator and evaluator to reject a correct sentence. Fix: drop the asterisk and recast as register: "deberías softens advice; debes is a firmer recommendation".
2. **es-b2-appreciative-suffixes, L7065 vs. L7094–7095:** the description's allomorph rule ("-c-/-e- after words ending in -n/-r or -e/ie-ue") does not generate several of the point's own seed values: florecita, solecito, vocecita and pececito (monosyllables, -ecito), and puentecito/cafecito. A model reconciling desc and seeds is pushed toward inventing rules. This is part of the same fix as the two audit rows above, but the seed/desc mismatch is its own defect.

Considered and not listed (not high-confidence):
- es-a2-tonic-pronouns-prepositions L2718 stars "*se le acercó". B&B §14.6.1 marks it `?*` but documents colloquial exceptions, including Peninsular literary ones (*Él se le acercó por la espalda*). The asterisk is overstated, but the book supports discouraging it.
- es-a2-estar-gerundio L2334 "*Estoy yendo al trabajo en metro." B&B §19.5 says Spain avoids the ir continuous for on-the-way motion. With an iterative "these days" reading, though, it is acceptable even in Spain (§19.2.4, *Está yendo mucho al cine estos días*). The example is ambiguous, not clearly wrong.
- es-b1-passive-se "Se le nota cansada": le with a feminine referent under impersonal se is standard (B&B §32 note 2, *se le considera útil*). This is not the feminine-leísmo defect.
